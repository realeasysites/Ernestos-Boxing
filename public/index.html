Meet the coach

Coach Ernesto

Ernesto's is built on the authentic, disciplined spirit of classic boxing, in a gym that feels like family.

Coach Ernesto opened the gym in Portland in October 2026 to bring old-school boxing, community and culture under one roof. Kids learn discipline, adults of all ages get a real challenge, and fighters get the work they need. Everyone gets the same respect.

Coach Ernesto joined WMPG 90.9 FM to talk about community, culture and boxing.
