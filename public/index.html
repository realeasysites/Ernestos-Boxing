Meet the coaches

Coach Ernesto

Ernesto Ornelas · USA Boxing-certified coach · Founder

Ernesto's is built on the authentic, disciplined spirit of classic boxing, in a gym that feels like family.

Coach Ernesto opened the gym in Portland's East Deering neighborhood in October 2026 to bring old-school boxing, community and culture under one roof. Kids learn discipline, adults of all ages get a real challenge, and fighters get the work they need. Everyone gets the same respect.

Coach Maureen Topa runs the gym day to day and leads the women's, functional boxing and conditioning classes.

  • Latino-owned
  • LGBTQ-friendly
  • Wheelchair accessible
  • Family-friendly
